I started out in Nepal writing Django and React Native at Prabidhi Labs, then moved to Oita, Japan in 2024 as a contract software engineer. Since then I've delivered 12+ projects for Japanese municipalities and enterprise clients — GIS portals, asset-management platforms, citizen apps and multi-district voucher systems.
