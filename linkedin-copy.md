copy-and-paste into the web form. Character limits are noted; LinkedIn silently
truncates past them.

### Worth doing: a Japanese profile version

LinkedIn supports multiple profile languages. Japanese recruiters searching in
Japanese will not find an English-only profile. Add one under
**Edit public profile & URL → Add profile in another language → 日本語**, then
translate the headline and About. It doubles your discoverability in the market
you actually work in.

## 9 · What NOT to put on LinkedIn

- **Client names.** Same rule as the portfolio — the municipalities and
  companies stay unnamed. "Japanese municipalities and enterprise clients" is
  enough, and it is what you can say safely.
- **Cloud architect framing.** You deploy and run on AWS; you are not selling
  yourself as an AWS specialist. The wording above keeps that honest.
